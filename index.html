What this is, and what it is not

A working prototype

Built in Botpress for the SIS 6223 NLP mini-project. A student can chat with it and a report is saved for the counsellor. Our tests also show where it still fails.

Synthetic data only

Every message we tested on was invented, drafted with AI and reviewed by people. Please do not type real names or details into the demo.

Emergency alerts are simulated

When MoSafety detects an emergency it shows an alert, but nothing is sent to anyone. A real alert needs a person on call.
