The chatbot

Talk to MoSafety

Write as you would to a school counsellor, in English, Français or Kreol. This is the real bot, running inside this page.

Slides 7 and 8: demo

Prototype. Use made-up scenarios only, and please do not type real names or personal details. Messages travel through a chatbot platform and an AI provider, and reports are saved to a table. Emergency alerts are : nothing is sent to anyone. A person makes every decision.

Your conversation stays in this browser until you choose Start over. It is not shown to anyone else on this site.

Not sure what to type?

Tap a sticky note and its message is sent for you. Each note says what a good reply looks like.

What a counsellor sees

Each saved report becomes one row, sorted so the most urgent cases come first.

The rows beside this text are invented to show the layout. They are not real reports. In the prototype the counsellor opens the report table in Botpress, because it does not send notifications yet. The real saved rows are on the register.

Known issues. In 9 live tests so far, 3 passed, 2 passed in part and 4 failed. The bot once repeated a slur back to the student, answered a Kreol message in English, and promised an alert or a counsellor call that does not really happen yet. It still calls itself “Shield-Link” in places, and a French message got a Kreol reply. We list every finding on the register and on Under the hood.
