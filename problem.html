Why can't Kavi just push back?

Because the weights are not equal. A is made of things like these:

  • Numbers. They are many. Kavi is one.
  • Fear. Of being named, and of making it worse.
  • Not being understood. Adults who seem far away, or a language that is not the one Kavi thinks in.
  • Waiting. Nothing seems to happen, so Kavi stops asking.

Four kinds, one pattern

MoSafety gives every message a nature. These are example messages from our invented test data.

Verbal

Words that hurt: names, threats, mockery.

“Someone called me a name in the corridor today.”

Physical

Hitting, pushing, breaking or taking things.

“A boy in Grade 9 Red pushed me in the corridor again today.”

Social

Leaving someone out, rumours, turning friends away.

“Nobody will be my partner because one boy told them not to.”

Cyber

Bullying through phones and apps.

“They keep making stickers of my face on WhatsApp.” (Kreol: Zot pe fer sticker mo figir lor WhatsApp toulezour.)

How big is it, and how often do students stay silent?

~1 in 3students worldwide were bullied at least once at school in the last monthUNESCO, Behind the Numbers, 2019, 144 countries and territories
22.2%of in-school adolescents in Mauritius reported being bullied on at least one of the previous 30 daysBMC Psychiatry, 2021, using WHO school health survey data from 2010 to 2017
44.2%of bullied US students aged 12 to 18 said an adult at school was notified. Fewer than half.NCES School Crime Supplement, 2021–22. We found no Mauritian figure for this.

These surveys use different definitions and different countries, so they cannot be compared directly. Together they show the scale of the problem and how often students say nothing.

What MoSafety is not

It does not stop bullying

It makes the first step easier and puts a clear report in front of a person. Everything after that is human work.

It is not a private diary

Messages travel through a chatbot platform and an AI provider whose terms we have not checked. We do not claim anonymity or legal compliance.

It cannot call anyone yet

The emergency alert and the counsellor hand-off are simulated. Nothing reaches a person until a school adds a real notification.
